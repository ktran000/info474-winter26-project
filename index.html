Why Do Some Countries Feel Happier Than Others?
Around the world, countries differ not only in wealth and health, but also in how people report their overall happiness. This interactive story explores global data to understand what factors are most closely connected to well-being — including economic strength, life expectancy, social support, and personal freedom.

Scroll to explore how these variables relate to happiness.
Exploring the Data
We begin by examining the relationship between economic prosperity and happiness. Does higher income per person correspond to higher reported well-being?
GDP and Happiness
This chart compares each country's logged GDP per capita with its average happiness score. Each point represents a country. If wealth strongly predicts happiness, we would expect to see a clear upward trend.
Beyond Wealth
Economic strength is only one piece of the puzzle. Next, we explore whether personal freedom — the ability to make life choices — is connected to happiness.
Freedom and Well-Being
This chart shows the relationship between freedom to make life choices and reported happiness. Hover over points to see individual countries. Notice whether higher freedom values tend to align with higher scores.
Health and Happiness
Life expectancy reflects overall health conditions, healthcare access, and quality of living. This visualization explores whether healthier populations report higher levels of happiness.
A Global Perspective
Instead of looking at individual relationships, we can visualize happiness geographically. The map below highlights countries by their happiness scores, revealing regional patterns around the world.
What We Learned
Happiness is shaped by multiple interconnected factors. While economic prosperity matters, social support, freedom, and health also play important roles. No single variable fully explains well-being — instead, happiness appears to be multidimensional, influenced by both material conditions and social environment.

Interactive visualization helps reveal these relationships in a clearer and more engaging way.
